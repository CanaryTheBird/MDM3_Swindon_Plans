Breaking down the divided areas of Swindon
1 - the town centre, includes the main shopping area as well as an outlet shopping area, the railway station and other local fixtures, e.g. football stadium.
Area 1 also features residential area with the only notable non-white population in Swindon, with a large Asian demographic
2 - the most well-off area of Swindon. Lowest levels of deprivation, highest rates of university education, near the motorway, even has a golf course
3, 7, 9, 11 - generally worse-off areas of Swindon. Tend to be older, high levels of economic inactivity, low levels of university degree qualification and higher levels of economic deprivation.
3 is the most deprived according to census, 7, 9, 11 are bad but not to same level as 3. These area are generally dull 1960s housing estates, on outskirts of town
4, 5, 6 - these are the main industrial estates, some of these mentioned in the project brief. 
Area 4 is South Marston, Area 5 is Chetney Manor, Area 6 is Kembrey Park with a retail park included in the south
8 - area more in line with the "average". A lot of new build housing, tends to skew younger, high levels of economic activity
10 - one of 2 junctions with the M4 (I may have cut the other one of the screenshot, but its in the southeast if you're curious).
